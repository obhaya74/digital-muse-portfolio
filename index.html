About

Saima Aslam Malik

Journalist · Anchorperson · Digital Creator

Digital Muse Creations is a digital media platform led by Saima Aslam Malik, a journalist and anchorperson with parliamentary reporting experience since 2007.

The platform pairs that background in public affairs, news presentation and audience communication with digital storytelling, video content and AI-assisted visual production, so public-interest information reaches people in formats that are engaging and easy to follow.

Based in Islamabad, Pakistan
